Consumer Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about cylinder weighment checks, leak emergencies, delivery SLAs, and new connection requirements.

Every RedFlame LPG delivery specialist carries a calibrated electronic hanging scale. Gross weight equals the tare weight permanently stamped on the cylinder collar (typically 15.3–15.8 kg) plus 14.2 kg net gas. Insist on weighment and verify the serialized holographic seal is completely intact before accepting.

Immediately switch off the cylinder regulator knob. Do not touch or operate any electrical switches, lamps, or phones. Open all doors and windows wide for ventilation. Extinguish all matchsticks or incense sticks, step outside the premises, and call the National Emergency LPG Helpline at 1906 immediately.

Domestic consumers can book a refill once every 15 days under government guidelines. Standard refills are dispatched within 24 hours, while our Express Delivery option guarantees doorstep fulfillment in under 2 hours across covered ward zones.

A new connection requires Aadhaar Card (for identity & biometric e-KYC), proof of residential address (Electricity Bill, Rent Agreement, or Voter ID), a bank account passbook for direct benefit subsidy transfer (DBTL), and passport photographs. e-KYC can be verified completely online.

Statutory guidelines require steel-wire reinforced ISI Suraksha hoses to be replaced every 5 years or immediately if cracks, stiffness, or rodent damage appear. Regulators should undergo mandatory inspection during the 5-year statutory safety audit or whenever loose locking collar play is noticed.
