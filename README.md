# RedFlame LPG — Gas Agency & Express Delivery Platform

Premium, reliable web platform for **RedFlame LPG Gas Agency & Delivery**, built with clean semantic HTML5, modern vanilla CSS3 design tokens, responsive bento grids, and interactive vanilla JavaScript.

## Website Features
- **Brand Identity**: RedFlame LPG (`Agency & Delivery`) with custom vector gas cylinder and combustion flame logo mark uniformly styled in colors, fonts, and sizes across all pages.
- **Theme & Colors**: Classic LPG Cylinder Red (`#DC2626` / `#B91C1C`), Flame Orange (`#EA580C` / `#F97316`), Dark Charcoal (`#1C1917` / `#120909`), and Soft Rose White (`#FEF2F2`).
- **Interactive Controls**: Light/Dark mode switcher, LTR/RTL text-direction toggle, and responsive mobile/tablet drawer navigation.
- **Delivery Promise**: 2-hour express dispatch SLA, calibrated electronic scale doorstep weighment check, and serialized holographic tamper-evident seal guarantee.

## Page Architecture & Single-Word Menu Structure
All menu items and file names (except Home and Home 2) use concise, single-word names:
- `index.html` — **Home** (Hero with delivery promise, quick booking strip, 4 distinction cards, 6 LPG services, 4-step journey, 4 delivery specialists, 4 tariff plans, and booking modal)
- `home-2.html` — **Home 2** (Express delivery experience, 6 bento cards, safety trust story, 4-step refill journey visual, delivery supervisor spotlight, 4 delivery benefits, and consumer portal preview)
- `agency.html` — **Agency** (Agency heritage, 4 metrics, mission & vision, 4 statutory safety distinctions, and trust CTA)
- `services.html` — **Services** (Domestic 14.2kg refills, commercial 19kg manifold supply, industrial 47.5kg bulk, new connection e-KYC, and mandatory 5-year kitchen safety audits)
- `tariff.html` — **Tariff** (Regulated cylinder pricing for 5kg, 14.2kg, 19kg, and 47.5kg cylinders, comprehensive inclusion matrix table, and doorstep weighment guarantees)
- `safety.html` — **Safety** (Essential LPG safety tips, emergency leak protocols, cylinder storage standards, regulator discipline, Suraksha hose inspection, and 24/7 hotline 1906)
- `support.html` — **Support** (24/7 emergency leak desk, customer care, refill grievance form, interactive central godown and regional ward depot map, and 5 FAQs)
- `dashboard.html` — **Dashboard** (Consumer dashboard for booking refills, live delivery tracking with assigned delivery specialist, digital weighment reports, and downloadable billing invoices)
- `login.html` — **Login** (Consumer portal authentication supporting 17-digit LPG ID / email sign-in and new account registration)

## Assets
- `assets/css/style.css` — Global design tokens, typography, navbar, footer, modals, and responsive layout.
- `assets/css/dashboard.css` — Consumer dashboard layout, sidebar drawer, stat cards, tables, and panes.
- `assets/js/main.js` — Navbar drawers, theme toggle, RTL switcher, refill modal calculation, and FAQ accordions.
- `assets/js/dashboard.js` — Consumer dashboard tab switching, live refill price calculator, order placement, and delivery updates.
- `assets/js/auth.js` — Consumer login/registration authentication, password visibility toggle, and session handling.
