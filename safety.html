Core Precautionary Practices

6 Essential LPG Safety Protocols

Adhering to certified safety rules keeps your home and business secure. Review these actionable steps recommended by PESO and national gas safety councils.

🚨 Immediate Action

Gas Leak Protocol

Emergency

Ethyl Mercaptan Odour Detection

  • No Electrical Sparks: Do not operate switches or phones
  • Cross Ventilation: Open all doors & windows wide
  • Regulator Off: Disconnect knob and call 1906 immediately
🛡️ Storage Standard

Storage & Placement

Ground Level

Level Flooring & Vapor Clearance

  • Upright Always: Never tilt, roll, or lay cylinder flat
  • Well Ventilated: Avoid locked cabinets or basements
  • Thermal Buffer: Maintain 1m clearance from hot appliances
🔒 Daily Routine

Regulator Routine

Mandatory

Shut-Off Discipline Before Sleep

  • Night Shutoff: Turn regulator knob completely off each night
  • Travel Precaution: Shut down valve when away from home
  • Locking Check: Verify collar clicks into collar ring firmly
🔍 Hardware Integrity

Suraksha Hose Care

5-Year Life

Steel Braided Anti-Rodent Mesh

  • BIS Certification: Use genuine Type-4 Suraksha hoses only
  • Visual Check: Inspect for cracking, stiffness, or cuts
  • Tight Clamps: Use stainless steel worm-drive clamps
⚖️ Verification SLA

Seal & Weight Check

Doorstep Check

Holographic Tamper-Proof Cap

  • Hologram Seal: Reject if plastic seal or cord is broken
  • Digital Scale: Insist on weighment test at delivery gate
  • Gross Math: Gross = Stamped Tare (15.5kg) + 14.2kg Gas
🔥 Combustion Standard

Burner Maintenance

Efficiency

Pristine Blue Flame Combustion

  • Pure Blue Flame: Orange or yellow flame indicates soot/dirt
  • Spill Cleanliness: Clean burner ports if milk or broth spills
  • Soap Bubble Test: Check valve joints with soapy water sponge
Statutory Standards

4 Statutory Pillars of Safe LPG Operations

PESO Certified Hardware

Every cylinder shell, self-closing valve, and low-pressure regulator strictly complies with Petroleum and Explosives Safety Organization and BIS standards.

Mandatory Safety Audits

Safety Protocols by Installation Tier

Different cooking environments need specialized precautions. Check our tailored protocols for residential homes and commercial establishments.

Residential Kitchens Daily Household Checklist

Domestic 14.2kg & 5kg Kitchen Safety

Recommended for residential apartments, independent homes, and compact kitchens. Maintain strict separation between LPG cylinder and electrical sockets, keep cooking stoves elevated above cylinder level, and ensure natural cross-ventilation.

Stove Above Cylinder 1.5m Hose Max Regulator Off at Night No Basements

Commercial 19kg & Industrial 47.5kg Banks

Recommended for restaurant kitchens, bakeries, cloud kitchens, and manufacturing units. Multi-cylinder manifold installations must have flame arrestors, external ventilated cylinder sheds, and emergency isolation valves accessible outside cooking halls.

Smell Gas? Act Immediately Without Hesitation.

Turn off your cylinder regulator immediately, do not operate any electrical appliances or light matches, open all windows, and dial the National LPG Emergency Leak Helpline 1906 or our direct agency response unit.
