About RedFlame LPG Agency

Powering Homes & Enterprises with Safe, Punctual Energy

Established to eliminate cooking gas delays, inaccurate cylinders, and safety risks. We serve over 150,000 households and commercial kitchens with certified LPG cylinders, electronic weight verification, and a guaranteed 2-hour delivery promise.

Our Origins

Eliminating the Kitchen Refill Crisis

Running out of LPG during Sunday lunch or mid-dinner preparation has long been a source of domestic panic. For decades, consumers dealt with opaque multi-day booking backlogs, arbitrary floor-carrying surcharges, and unverified cylinder weights with broken seals.

RedFlame LPG Agency was established to transform cooking fuel logistics into a modern, transparent utility. Operating with licensed PESO standards and localized neighborhood godowns, we deliver certified cylinders within two hours, verified by calibrated scales right at your doorway.

Our Philosophy

More Than a Distributorship — Your Safety Partner

Gas cylinder delivery is never just about carrying metal; it is a solemn commitment to household safety. We believe every homemaker, chef, and industrial manager deserves total confidence that their gas installation is airtight, calibrated, and compliant.

We equip all delivery personnel with calibrated digital scales, O-ring replacement kits, and soap leak testers, conducting a complimentary valve safety audit on every single refill exchange.
